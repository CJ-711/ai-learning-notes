`Turn a small fitness product into a clear social video`

- TikTok
- Instagram Reels
- YouTube Shorts

比例：9:16

时长：15 秒

定位：产品场景短视频，不写成真实广告投放结果。

## 2. 15 秒结构

| 时间 | 画面 | 字幕 | 目的 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0-3s | 产品套装清晰出现 | `Small product, clear use case.` | 让观众立即知道是什么 |
| 3-6s | 办公桌使用场景 | `Desk break: 60 seconds.` | 把功能转成日常场景 |
| 6-10s | 健身后使用场景 | `Home workout add-on.` | 扩展目标用户 |
| 10-13s | 指力带/配件演示 | `Show the action, not just the feature.` | 展示可拍性 |
| 13-15s | 产品 + CTA | `Test the content before inventory.` | 回到跨境运营判断 |

## 3. 可灵 / Runway B-roll 提示词

### Shot 01：产品英雄镜头

```
Create a realistic 9:16 vertical product video for a small fitness accessory kit.

Subject:
A compact hand grip trainer kit on a clean desk, including a hand gripper, finger resistance band, and grip ring.

Scene:
Clean home office desk, neutral background, natural daylight, product centered and clearly visible.

Camera:
Slow push-in from medium close-up to close-up, steady camera, no dramatic movement.

Style:
Realistic social media product demo, practical, clean, not cinematic, not luxury.

Requirements:
- No text or logo generated by AI.
- Keep the product shape consistent.
- No extra products or confusing props.
- Leave clean space at top and bottom for subtitles.
- 3 seconds, 9:16 vertical.
```

### Shot 02：办公桌间隙使用

```
Create a realistic 9:16 vertical short video scene.

Subject:
A person uses a compact hand grip trainer during a short desk break.

Scene:
Home office desk with laptop, notebook, water bottle, and simple fitness product.

Action:
The person picks up the grip trainer and uses it naturally for a few repetitions.

Camera:
Close-up on hand and product, slight handheld movement, product remains clear.

Style:
Natural, practical, TikTok / Instagram Reels product demo.

Requirements:
- No readable screen content.
- No exaggerated fitness result.
- No medical, pain relief, or therapy claim.
- Leave clean space for captions.
- 3 seconds, 9:16 vertical.
```

### Shot 03：居家健身补充场景

```
Create a 9:16 vertical product use scene for a home workout audience.

Subject:
A beginner fitness user uses a hand grip trainer after a light home workout.

Scene:
Simple home workout corner with yoga mat, towel, water bottle, and soft daylight.

Action:
The person sits down after a workout and uses the grip trainer naturally.

Camera:
Medium close-up, then slow move to hands using the product.

Style:
Realistic, beginner-friendly, practical social media content.

Requirements:
- No before/after body transformation.
- No medical or recovery claim.
- Product should remain recognizable.
- Leave clean space for subtitles.
- 4 seconds, 9:16 vertical.
```

### Shot 04：配件动作演示

```
Create a realistic 9:16 vertical close-up demo.

Subject:
Hands using a finger resistance band from a compact grip trainer kit.

Scene:
Clean tabletop with simple fitness accessories, bright natural light.

Action:
Hands stretch the finger resistance band slowly so the movement is easy to understand.

Camera:
Top-down close-up, stable framing, product and hand action clearly visible.

Style:
Instructional short video, clear and practical.

Requirements:
- No text generated by AI.
- No extra fingers or distorted hands.
- No medical claim.
- Leave clean space for captions.
- 3 seconds, 9:16 vertical.
```

## 4. CapCut 剪辑步骤

1. 建 9:16 项目。
2. 导入 4 个 B-roll 镜头和当前产品图。
3. 每个镜头控制在 2-4 秒。
4. 前 3 秒必须出现产品和字幕，不要空镜。
5. 添加英文大字幕：
   - `Small product, clear use case.`
   - `Desk break: 60 seconds.`
   - `Home workout add-on.`
   - `Show the action, not just the feature.`
   - `Test content before inventory.`
6. 添加轻音乐，不盖过字幕节奏。
7. 导出 1080x1920 MP4。
8. 保存一版中文讲解版，用于面试讲解。

## 5. 质量检查

发布或放进作品集前检查：

- 产品是否在 3 秒内被看懂。
- 手部是否畸形，产品是否变形。
- AI 是否生成了奇怪文字、Logo 或假数据。
- 是否避免医疗、康复、疼痛缓解、保证效果。
- 字幕是否自然，是否像海外社媒内容。
- 是否能解释每个镜头对应的运营目标。
- 是否标明 AI-assisted，不暗示真实客户拍摄。

## 6. 面试讲法

> 这个 CJ Sport 视频不是为了证明真实销量，而是展示我如何用 AI 视频工具提高产品内容生产效率。我会先写产品 brief 和 15 秒结构，再用可灵或 Runway 做 3-4 个产品场景 B-roll，最后在 CapCut 里加字幕、节奏和 CTA。对我来说，AI 视频的重点不是炫技，而是更快测试不同使用场景和 Hook。

如果面试官问“你会不会用可灵”：

> 我会把可灵当作产品场景 B-roll 工具。提示词会控制产品主体、场景、镜头、动作和禁用表达。比如这个握力器案例，我不会让 AI 生成夸张健身效果，而是生成办公桌间隙、居家训练、配件演示这些可解释的短镜头，再用 CapCut 完成字幕和最终剪辑。
