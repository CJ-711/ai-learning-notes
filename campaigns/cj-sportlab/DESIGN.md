# CJ Sport Visual Direction

## Brand

CJ Sport is the first sports product line under the broader CJ personal brand. The first test product is a Grip Trainer Kit. GripLab is no longer used as the public brand name; it can remain an internal product-testing concept if needed.

## Visual Identity

- Mood: compact, performance-driven, ecommerce-ready.
- Canvas: dark graphite background with neon lime and cool blue accents.
- Palette:
  - Graphite: `#080a08`
  - Charcoal: `#151b17`
  - Lime: `#c9ff00`
  - Cool blue: `#2ac2ff`
  - White: `#f5f7ef`
- Typography: system sans-serif, heavy uppercase headlines, short utility labels.

## Messaging Rules

- Do not make medical, therapy, pain relief, or rehabilitation claims.
- Do not promise fast transformation.
- Use honest product-test language: sample, sourcing, routine, test, beginner, desk-friendly.
- Make the product large before adding data. TikTok needs fast recognition; Amazon needs clear product and bullet hierarchy.

## Campaign Line

Buy cheap, test honestly, then decide.
