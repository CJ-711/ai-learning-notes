## Regeneration Standard

Regenerate any image if it fails one of these checks:

- Hands have distorted fingers, impossible grip posture, or product scale feels
  wrong.
- The product identity changes from black/neon lime CJ Sport Lab.
- The scene looks like a stock product mockup instead of a real use moment.
- Logos are misspelled or visibly warped.
- The product floats on top of a scene instead of physically interacting with the
  hand, bag, desk, or training surface.
