# Source and License Notes

## Allowed Source Type

Use:
- stock footage with clear commercial-use terms
- AI-generated product renders created for CJ Sport Lab
- owned future footage
- self-made motion graphics, captions, logos, and overlays

Avoid:
- ripped TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Nike, adidas, Gymshark, Under Armour, influencer, or competitor ads
- videos containing visible third-party trademarks
- athlete likenesses without permission
- editorial-only footage for product promotion

## Mixkit

Mixkit pages for the downloaded clips state that the videos can be downloaded for commercial or personal use under the Mixkit Stock Video Free License. Source URLs are stored in `asset-manifest.csv`.

Keep the CSV with the project so future edits can trace each clip.

## Pexels

Pexels has relevant grip, resistance band, office, and basketball clips, but the site blocked automated page downloads during this run. Keep Pexels as a manual/browser fallback or use its official API if an API key is available.

## Practical Rule

The stock clips should support context and movement. The CJ Sport Lab product must be generated, owned, or clearly licensed separately. Do not imply the stock athlete is endorsing CJ Sport Lab.
