# CJ Sport Lab Grip Trainer Ad Storyboard

## Working Title

CJ Sport Lab | Grip Strength Anywhere

## Format

- Platform: Instagram Reels / TikTok
- Ratio: 9:16
- Target length: 18-24 seconds
- Language: English first
- Style: realistic product ad, fast-cut, practical, athletic, black/neon green accents

## Core Message

You do not need a full gym session to start training. A small grip tool can turn small breaks into useful daily training moments.

## Beat Structure

### 0.0-2.5s: Hook

Visual:
- Vertical office hands clip.
- Desk, keyboard, hand fatigue, small pause.
- Product render appears or cuts in as if picked up from the desk.

Caption:
`Your hands train before your body moves.`

VO:
`Training does not always start in the gym.`

### 2.5-6.0s: Problem

Visual:
- Desk hand close-up.
- Fast insert of basketball court or training context.
- Hand, wrist, forearm, ball/control implied.

Caption:
`Grip. Control. Consistency.`

VO:
`For everyday athletes, grip strength shows up everywhere.`

### 6.0-11.5s: Product Use

Visual:
- AI-generated CJ Sport Lab grip trainer in hand.
- Stock hand/desk footage used as lifestyle base.
- Product macro still/video with neon green accent.
- Short squeeze motion simulated with scale/mask if no real video exists.

Caption:
`Small tool. Real reps.`

VO:
`CJ Sport Lab turns small breaks into simple, repeatable training.`

### 11.5-17.5s: Sport Context

Visual:
- Basketball court training.
- Treadmill/gym/indoor workout footage.
- Product shown in bag or before training, not floating as a fake object.

Caption:
`Before the court. Before the run. Before the next rep.`

VO:
`Use it at work, at home, or before the session.`

### 17.5-22.0s: Brand Close

Visual:
- CJ Sport Lab product hero render.
- Logo reveal over dark background with neon green rim light.

Caption:
`CJ Sport Lab`

Subcaption:
`Move Small. Train Smart.`

VO:
`CJ Sport Lab. Move small. Train smart.`

## Required New Product Assets

The current stock clips do not show the exact CJ Sport Lab grip trainer. Generate or source:

1. Product hero image, transparent background.
2. Product in hand, office desk.
3. Product in hand, sports bag / court edge.
4. Macro spring/handle close-up.
5. Optional 3-5 second AI video clip of squeezing the grip trainer.

## What Not To Do

- Do not show long brand explanations.
- Do not use fake data dashboards.
- Do not use random sport montage without product logic.
- Do not make the product float over unrelated video.
- Do not use unlicensed brand ads or athlete footage.
