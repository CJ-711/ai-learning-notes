## Voiceover Direction

The new VO should feel like an overseas short-form product director wrote it:
shorter lines, more movement, less corporate brand explanation.

### v7 Office Product Use

Your day is packed. That doesn't mean training has to disappear. One call ends,
one small reset starts. Grip a few reps at the desk. Open the fingers, shake out
the hands, keep the rhythm moving. CJ Sport Lab makes compact tools for the gaps
in real life. Small reps. Real consistency. Move small. Train smart.

### v8 Commute Pocket Training

You don't need a perfect workout window to start. Waiting for the train, walking
out of the office, packing your bag. Those are the moments CJ Sport Lab is built
for. Small tools, easy carry, quick hand work wherever the day takes you. Pack
it. Use it. Repeat it. Move small. Train smart.

## Quality Judgment

v7 is currently stronger than v8 because it shows the product being used more
often and the office-break story is clearer. v8 works as a compact-carry route,
but the next improvement should be true commute-use material: a hand using the
grip ring or grip trainer while waiting, standing, or sitting during commute.

The current videos are still animated keyframe edits, not real product-motion
footage. For final paid-ad quality, generate or film two-state hand movement:
open grip, squeezed grip, release, and pack-away.
