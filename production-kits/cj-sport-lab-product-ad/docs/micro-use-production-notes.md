# CJ Sport Lab Micro-Use Production Notes

## Current Direction

The next CJ Sport Lab cuts should sell a specific behavior, not a broad sports mood:

> small grip tools used around desk work, commuting, bags, and warmups.

The user feedback was clear: because the product is a set of small sport tools, the video should show believable small moments where those tools fit. Office workers at a desk, someone packing a bag, a commuter waiting or holding a subway handle, and a phone-in-hand walk are stronger than a generic basketball or gym montage.

## Selected Creative Production Path

- Scenes: realistic product-ad contexts where the tool can fit naturally.
- Shots: close hands, keyboard/mouse fatigue, bag packing, phone/pocket, subway handle, product macro.
- Ads: 9:16 TikTok/Reels product cutdowns with hard hooks and short captions.

Figma is not required for this pass because the deliverable is rendered video, not a static layout system. Notion capture needs a destination page/database before writing externally; the repo notes remain the source of truth for this pass.

## Product Specificity Boundary

The current stock footage does not show the real CJ Sport Lab tools in hand. These cuts should therefore be honest in their construction:

- use stock footage for context and motion;
- use CJ Sport Lab generated product images for product truth;
- use fast insert cuts between the two;
- avoid fake compositing that implies the stock actor is holding the product.

The next quality jump should come from generated or owned footage of:

- a hand squeezing the black/neon adjustable grip trainer at a desk;
- a finger extensor used near a laptop;
- the ring or knuckle trainer inside a work bag;
- a short subway/elevator wait with the tool held naturally.
