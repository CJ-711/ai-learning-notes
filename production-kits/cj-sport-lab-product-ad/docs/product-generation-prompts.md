# Product Generation Prompts

Use these prompts in ChatGPT image/video generation or another tool that can produce realistic product visuals.

## Product Identity

Product:
Compact hand grip trainer for everyday athletes.

Brand look:
Matte black body, neon lime green accent, minimal CJ Sport Lab mark, premium sports-tech feeling.

Avoid:
Big fake text, unreadable labels, extra logos, deformed fingers, impossible springs, cartoon render, plastic toy look.

## Prompt 1: Transparent Product Hero

```text
Realistic studio product photography of a compact hand grip trainer for a modern sports brand.
Matte black ergonomic handles, neon lime green spring accent, subtle CJ Sport Lab logo mark on the handle, premium athletic product design.
Three-quarter angle, high detail, realistic rubber and metal texture, soft rim lighting, black background, isolated product, no hand, no extra text, no watermark.
```

Output need:
- PNG if possible
- transparent background preferred
- 1080x1080 or higher
