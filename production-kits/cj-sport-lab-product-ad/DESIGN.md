# CJ Sport Lab Video Design

## Style Prompt

Premium compact sport-tech for overseas short-form social. The product should feel practical, carryable, and used in small daily windows: desk breaks, commute waiting, bag carry, and pre-training warmups. The visual tone is cinematic but grounded, with realistic office and transit footage, tight product inserts, fast pacing, and minimal copy.

## Colors

- Background: `#050605` near-black, slightly green-tinted
- Foreground: `#F5F7EF` warm white
- Accent: `#C9FF00` CJ neon lime
- Secondary dark: `#11160F` charcoal green-black
- Muted text: `#AAB3A2` grey-green

## Typography

- Display: Bahnschrift, heavy/condensed, all caps for short hooks
- Body: Segoe UI, light to regular, sentence case for support copy

## Motion Rules

- Use fast close cuts, 2.4-3.4 second scenes, and mobile push-ins.
- Use fade/flash handoffs instead of hard slideshow cuts.
- Keep captions to one short line plus one optional support phrase.
- Product stills can pulse or push in, but should not pretend to be real live footage.

## What Not To Do

- Do not make a generic sports montage where the product is incidental.
- Do not use long text blocks, data dashboards, or presentation cards.
- Do not imply stock actors endorse CJ Sport Lab.
- Do not make medical, rehab, pain-relief, or guaranteed strength claims.
